How these rankings and percentages work

This app uses a custom heuristic, not solver results, measured equity, or an optimal playing range. It ranks starting hands without position, opponents, betting action, rake or tournament pressure. Rankings are illustrative and can differ from strategy charts.

The base score uses high-card points (A=10, K=8, Q=7, J=6, numbered cards=half their rank). Pairs receive twice their high-card points, with a minimum of 5. Nonpairs get +2 for suitedness; gaps cost 0, 1, 2, 4 or 5 points for zero, one, two, three or more missing ranks. Low connected or one-gap hands receive +1. The deep-stack factor is log(stack/20) divided by log(15), clipped from −0.5 to 1. At deeper stacks, suited hands gain 0.8–1.8 points, connected hands below Q gain up to 1.8 points, pairs below T gain 1.5 points, and offsuit hands lose 0.5–1.3 points. Short stacks reverse these adjustments. Pairs 22–99 also receive an extra short-stack bonus: +3 points at 5 BB, +1.5 at 10 BB, tapering logarithmically to zero at 20 BB and above. These weights are design assumptions, not validated expected values. Ties favour higher cards, then suitedness.

Click a box to select a hand, then move it up or down 1 or 10 ranks. Moves stop at the first or last rank and keep the other hands in order. Rankings stay fixed within each group: 5–10, 11–20, 21–30, 31–50, 51–75, 76–100, 101–130, 131–160, 161–200, 201–250 and 251–300 BB. Each group uses its lowest stack size for the default heuristic order. Custom edits apply to every stack in the group and save in this browser on this device. Reset this group restores its default order. Other groups retain their own orders. Older per-stack edits migrate using the lowest edited stack in each group.

There are 1,326 equally likely two-card combinations in a fresh deck: 6 per pair, 4 per suited hand, and 12 per offsuit hand. Each grid box shows the cumulative probability of being dealt that hand or any stronger-ranked hand, including every combination of all preceding hands. Clicking a hand sets the top-hands slider to that cumulative percentage and highlights that hand and all higher-ranked hands. The range slider selects the closest whole-hand prefix in ranking order. “Keep” buttons multiply the target slider percentage by the button percentage, rounded to two decimals and capped at 100%. Lock the current effective stack into A and B, then tap either saved slot to switch. Slots stay fixed until you lock a new value into them and save on this device. Below 100% grid size, boxes show only the starting hand; full rank and probability remain available in the accessible label. The aggression slider scales every preset by up to ±20% of its base range. Stack changes move aggression linearly from +10% at 5 BB to −10% at 300 BB; aggression changes never alter stack size. If a position preset is active, its selected range follows aggression changes. This is a custom adjustment rule, not solver guidance. Save customised app downloads an HTML copy with all edited rankings and current settings embedded; open that saved copy to retain them independently of browser storage. The actual percentage can be slightly above or below your target; 0% fades all hands and 100% highlights all hands. The red/orange/yellow/green/blue bands correspond to cumulative 0–5% (bright red), 5–10% (red), 10–20%, 20–30%, 30–50%, and 50–100% of dealt combinations in the selected ranking order. Excluded hands remain visible but faded.
